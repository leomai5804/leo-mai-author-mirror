ABOUT THE BOOK

A political allegory spanning an imperial court, a desert, and a lost civilization.

In a culturally hybrid empire, the ruler fears death, ministers weaponize belief, and court ritual conceals a machinery of violence. Prophetic dreams, political conspiracies, forbidden knowledge, religious figures, and observers from beyond the empire gradually converge.

Red Snow fuses imperial politics, religious allegory, and speculative fiction to ask what remains of human nature when power has no limit. The private desires of emperors, heirs, concubines, ministers, rebels, and witnesses become inseparable from public catastrophe.
