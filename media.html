AUTHOR BIO

Leo Mai

Leo Mai is a writer concerned with history, power, and the human condition. He lets the work speak before the biography: Red Snow confronts a civilization consumed by desire, while Flowers in the Ashes preserves a generation's ideals and wounds. Across two distinct literary worlds, his work asks one enduring question: when immense forces enter an individual life, can memory, moral choice, and dignity survive?
